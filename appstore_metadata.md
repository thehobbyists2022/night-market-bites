## Description (paste as-is)
```
Night Market Bites turns your kitchen into seven night markets at once. Taiwan, Thailand, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, the Philippines and Vietnam — more than 130 real street-food dishes, all in one app.

Every dish comes from the actual night markets and bunsik shops of Asia: tteokbokki and gua bao, khao soi and hoy tod, takoyaki and okonomiyaki, nasi lemak and laksa, halo-halo and fishballs, banh xeo and caphe sua da — plus the popcorn chicken that rules Taiwanese night markets.

HOW IT WORKS
• The Market Hall lists seven districts — one per country. Walk into a district, browse its stalls, cook the real thing.
• Each recipe is written from the counter up: local name with pronunciation, key flavors, exact ratios, and the substitutions real home cooks use.
• Master steps with large step text and a duration for every step, so nothing burns while you read.

HIGHLIGHTS
• 130+ authentic recipes across 7 countries, each with country-correct categories (bunsik, yakimono, konamono, pasar sweets and more)
• Local Order Card: the native name and phonetic pronunciation for every dish, so you can confidently order or shop for wee bits
• Metric & US units: switch every ingredient between g/ml and oz/cup
• Taste Passport: track the stalls you have toured across all seven countries
• Pocket List: save favorites from any country into one cross-country list
• 8 languages: English first, switching to Traditional Chinese, Thai, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Filipino, Vietnamese
• Every recipe shows prep+cook time, servings, calories, and difficulty

PRIVACY FIRST: no account, no sign-up, no tracking. Your passport stamps and pocket list stay on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

DISCOVERY MADE EASY
Filter each district by its own authentic street-food categories, or jump straight into any stall from the district header. Substitutions are built into the ingredient cards so hard-to-find items never block your cooking.

NOTES
Night Market Bites is an independent educational cooking companion. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any restaurant, brand, or food manufacturer. Recipes are for educational purposes — please follow safe food-handling and cooking temperatures (see the in-app guidance and our Privacy Policy page).
```
