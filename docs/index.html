Night Market Bites

Walk into a night market: recipes, stall guides and hands-free cooking timers for the street food of Taiwan, Thailand, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, the Philippines and Vietnam.

About the app

Night Market Bites is an independent, educational cooking companion that combines the street food of seven Asian countries in one app. It works without any account, and everything you save stays on your device.

130+ Authentic RecipesReal night-market dishes from all seven countries, with step timers.
Seven DistrictsEach country is a market district with its stalls, categories and stories.
Local Order CardThe local name and pronunciation for every dish, so you can order with confidence.
Metric & US UnitsSwitch every ingredient between g/ml and oz/cup.
Taste PassportTrack the stalls you have toured and keep a cross-country pocket list.
8 LanguagesEnglish first, with Thai, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Filipino and Vietnamese.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Night Market Bites works fully without any sign-up. All of your passport stamps and pocket-list favorites are stored only on your device.

Does it collect my data?

No. We do not collect personal data and we do not track your location. See our Privacy Policy.

Which countries are included?

Taiwan, Thailand, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, the Philippines and Vietnam — over 130 street recipes in total.

Which languages are supported?

The app opens in English and switches anytime to Traditional Chinese, Thai, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Filipino or Vietnamese.

Some ingredients are hard to find. What now?

Key ingredients include substitution suggestions right on the ingredient card.
