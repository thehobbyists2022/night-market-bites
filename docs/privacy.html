Critical Compliance & Safety Disclaimer

Privacy Policy & Food Safety Terms

Night Market Bites · Last updated: September 2026

Summary: Night Market Bites is a privacy-first, offline culinary companion app. We DO NOT collect personal information. Every recipe, favorite, passport stamp, language setting and unit preference is stored 100% locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No Account Required: You can explore all recipes, market districts, local order cards and timers without creating an account or logging in.
  • No Sensitive Personal Data: We do not ask for, harvest, or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or camera feed.
  • No Financial or Payment Data Storage: We do not process payments or credit card information.

2. Local Storage & Device Preferences

To ensure our offline-first experience and personalised recipes, Night Market Bites stores the following data locally on your device using localStorage:

  • Display language: your chosen interface language (English, Traditional Chinese, Thai, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Filipino, Vietnamese).
  • Selected cooking units: Metric (g / ml) or US (oz / cup).
  • Taste Passport data: which stalls you have toured and your cross-country pocket-list favorites.

You can always inspect this data or clear it through your browser or device storage settings.

3. Affiliate & Third-Party Retailer Links

Night Market Bites may contain links that help you locate authentic ingredients such as gochujang, fish sauce, rice noodles, or coconut milk. If you choose to purchase using these links, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.

Amazon Associates Program: As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases when you click marked ingredient links and complete a purchase. Participating retailers include Amazon.com and other licensed marketplaces. All core app features remain fully available whether or not you use these links.

4. Food Safety & Cooking Disclaimer

The recipes provided in Night Market Bites are intended for general food preparation and educational purposes only.

  • Appliance Settings Vary: Air fryer, pressure cooker, and microwave settings differ by brand, model, and wattage. Always follow the manufacturer's manual for liquid ratios, venting, and pressure-release procedures. Start with the shorter cooking time and adjust as needed.
  • Hot Oil & Spatter Caution: Always exercise extreme caution when heating or deep-frying oil. Keep a safe distance, use appropriate tools, and keep children and pets away from the cooking area.
  • Nutrition Disclaimer: Calorie and nutrition values shown in this app are approximations provided for convenience. Do not use them as a replacement for professional medical or dietary advice. Always consult a licensed nutritionist or physician for specific dietary, medical, or health-related questions. By using the recipes in this app, you agree to do so at your own risk; Night Market Bites and Clarity Clinical Solutions LLC accept no liability for injury, illness, or loss arising from improper food storage, handling, or preparation.
  • Dietary Modifications: Some recipes may need to be adapted for allergies, gluten intolerance (celiac disease), or vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, low-sodium, nut-free, or low-sugar lifestyles. Ingredient substitutions and dietary guidance may need to be adjusted based on individual needs.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. It is designed for general audiences, culinary exploration, and healthy home cooking.
